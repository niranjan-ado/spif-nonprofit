Equal Opportunity Policy

Suicide Prevention India Foundation (“SPIF”) is an equal opportunity employer committed to sound, ethical practices across all organizational workflows, social initiatives, and employment operations.

1. Our Commitment 2. Policy Definition 3. Governance Framework 4. Policy Guidance
handshake

handshake 1. Our Commitment to Individual Respect

Our continued growth and systemic success depend fundamentally on respect for the individual and providing all team members with the exact same opportunity to learn, advance, and succeed.

This core principle applies unilaterally to all scopes of employment, including recruitment, hiring processes, specialized training arrays, promotions, compensation structures, benefit allocations, transfers, organizational terminations, and social or recreational programs.

We firmly believe that equal opportunities cultivate a workspace ecosystem where employees progress strictly on the basis of capability, merit, and talent. This is best realized with a dedicated workforce that values, respects, and actively reflects the natural diversity of our broader society.

Why Fairness Drives Impact

When professionals feel structurally valued, safe, and treated with absolute fairness, organizational commitment increases. Eliminating bias allows SPIF to nurture a highly creative, collaborative, and productive environment focused entirely on our life-saving mandates.

balance

balance 2. What equal opportunity means at SPIF

Equal opportunity means that all employees are treated with uniform dignity and are never systematically disadvantaged or marginalized by underlying prejudices, stereotypes, or subjective bias.

SPIF strictly prohibits and maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy toward any form of unlawful discrimination, whether direct or indirect, across any employment opportunity or administrative practice.

Protected Characteristics Matrix

No corporate action or practice shall discriminate based upon: race, color, religion, religious belief, observance and practice, sex (including gender, gender identity, gender expression, pregnancy, childbirth, and medical conditions related to pregnancy), national origin, citizenship, age, marital status, sexual orientation, physical or mental disability, legally protected medical conditions, or any other status protected under applicable state or national legislation.

account_tree

account_tree 3. Governance & Operational Frameworks

To ensure this policy is actively enforced, SPIF has established the following operational frameworks and responsibilities across all levels of the organization:

  • The employee's role in maintaining workspace equality Standard Conduct
  • Ensuring equal opportunities throughout recruitment pipelines Talent Acquisition
  • Long-term structural alignment frameworks in daily employment Retention Protocols
  • Supervisor, lead handler, and administrative responsibilities Leadership Mandate
  • Official complaint procedures and grievance filing structures Internal Redressal
  • Comprehensive non-retaliation legal protections Anti-Retaliation
