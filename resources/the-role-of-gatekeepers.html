The Role of Gatekeepers

Suicide is a serious public health crisis. However, suicides are preventable. The key is early identification of warning signs and timely interventions.

People who attempt suicide give us some warning signs or clues when they are thinking about ending their life. A friend, family member, co-worker or classmate is likely to be the first person to notice suicide warning signs in their loved ones and can act as a major buffer against suicide. These ‘first responders’ are known as Gatekeepers - anyone in a position to identify whether someone is contemplating suicide.

A Gatekeeper can be:

  • school A teacher Who observes a sudden dramatic change in behaviour of a student in her class. The otherwise regular student started missing school, stays aloof, picks frequent fights with fellow classmates and remains irritable.
  • favorite A friend Who notices that her loved one keeps expressing that living life is like a burden to her and feels that everyone’s life would get better if she isn’t around.
  • work A colleague Who identifies that his fellow co-worker has suddenly started drinking too much and is failing deadlines, has stopped communicating with others and has unexplained anger towards everyone.

Warning signs Gatekeepers should watch out for:

The first step in preventing suicide is to identify the warning signs which may come in several different forms. Warning signs for suicide are defined as the earliest detectable indication of increased risk for suicide in the near future be it within minutes, hours or days.

The mere presence of one or two signs does not indicate suicide but it is important to identify the warning signs which suggest acute distress, despair, or hopelessness about the future.

verified_user Apply QPR

It is similar in its approach with CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation). They both rely on early identification of warning signs and providing necessary intervention. QPR is designed to save a life through three key skills: Question, Persuade and Refer. A Gatekeeper only needs to identify that some level of suicide risk is present and apply QPR, ensuring a referral is made to an appropriate professional.
