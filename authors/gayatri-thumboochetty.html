Gayatri Thumboochetty

Demographic Researcher & Trend Analyst

Demographic Mapping Legislative Tracking Public Health Data Statistical Synthesis
link Connect on LinkedIn

Gayatri Thumboochetty serves as a core research contributor for the Suicide Prevention India Foundation, specializing in public health data aggregation, national demographic trends, and legislative framework tracking. She maps macro variations in self-harm rates across specific age cohorts, occupations, and geographic regions within India.

Her analytical work provides a data-driven foundation for SPIF's educational models. By synthesizing records from public registries and global health databases, Gayatri uncovers the broad socioeconomic and administrative structures that directly influence mental health crises among vulnerable populations.

Mapping Macro Trends and Policy Evolution

Gayatri's primary research focuses on compiling and examining youth and student self-harm statistics across the sub-continent. Her investigative overviews analyze shifting trends inside registries like the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB)—tracking exactly how academic stress, familial complications, and economic variables scale into national vulnerabilities.

Concurrently, she monitors major structural policy milestones, evaluating the real-world impact of India's Mental Healthcare Act 2017 and the decriminalization of suicide attempts. Her work highlights critical administrative gaps—such as the operational demand for continuous 24/7 helpline access during nighttime hours and the modernization of academic training pathways for psychologists—offering a blueprint for systemic, community-wide gatekeeping.

Reviewing Public Health Statistics

Interested in reading how epidemiological registries track public health data to design structural safety frameworks? Explore these authorized data archives:

  • National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) Portal — Review official annual reports on accidental deaths and suicide trends within India.
  • Explore the SPIF Insights Index — Read Gayatri's full investigative catalog detailing macro trends, student data, and policy transformations.

Empowering Communities with Empirical Clarity

Ultimately, Gayatri's research seeks to transform passive statistics into human impact. By documenting the exact scope of public health concerns, her writing builds awareness and underlines a critical reality: crises are preventable through timely, structured, and low-cost community interventions, empowering everyday citizens to build proactive circles of protection.
