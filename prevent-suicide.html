You have the power to Prevent Suicide.

We work to deploy a decentralized circle of global protection across India. By equipping everyday citizens, workplace administrators, and clinical teams with evidence-supported response pathways, we actively safeguard vulnerable populations.

format_quote format_quote

"In many tracking environments where a vulnerable individual did not complete self-harm paths, three key protective buffers were documented—integrated domestic systems, close social support nets, and spiritual frameworks, playing an absolute frontline role in overall de-escalation."

Dr. Nimesh Desai

Psychiatrist & Former Director, IHBAS
