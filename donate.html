Get Involved with SPIF

Virtual Internships: If you are a student, we offer options to intern virtually for a minimum of one month across research, writing, social media, curriculum development, marketing, and design frameworks.

Civic Volunteering: We are constantly looking for self-motivated volunteers of any age group passionate about suicide prevention. No prior training or psychological diagnostic experience is required.

Organize a Workshop: Help us improve regional mental health literacy by organizing an awareness talk or a custom certified gatekeeper training program inside your company, apartment complex, school, or college network.

Ready to apply your skills? Write to our admin desk with your CV and desired target role.
